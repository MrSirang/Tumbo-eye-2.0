Project: Tumbo Website Development
Prepared by: Reabetswe Mothiba

Purpose
This document summarises the outcomes of the internal website review meeting held 
on 28 July 2026. Its purpose is to provide structured feedback on the current state of 
the website, identify outstanding work, clarify responsibilities, and guide the next 
phase of development.
Overall, the team is satisﬁed with the progress made to date. The website has 
established a strong foundation in terms of design, structure, and functionality. 
However, several areas require reﬁnement before the platform proceeds to the next 
phase of development.
It is important to note that several outstanding items depend on organisational 
content that has not yet been provided. These items remain outside the developer's 
scope until the required information has been supplied.
Overall Assessment
The current website demonstrates signiﬁcant progress and aligns well with the approved 
project scope. The website structure, navigation, visual design, and responsiveness provide a 
solid foundation for the Tumbo platform.
The next development phase should focus on:
Incorporating accurate organisational content.
Completing outstanding frontend functionality.
Developing backend functionality.
Reﬁning the user experience.

Additional Application Requirements
1. Opportunity Redirection Functionality
To improve the application experience and increase successful submissions, the platform 
should provide a seamless transition from opportunity discovery to application.
Requirements
When users select an opportunity, they should be redirected directly to the official 
application website, portal or online application form.
Where organisations have integrated directly with the platform, completed applications 
should be submitted directly through the system.
External links should:
Open securely.
Clearly notify users that they are leaving the Tumbo platform.
Redirect users to the official opportunity provider.
2. Opportunity Disclaimer
Disclaimer
The Tumbo platform connects users with veriﬁed employment, education, funding, 
internship, bursary and development opportunities.
===== PAGE 9 =====
While every effort is made to ensure opportunities are accurate and current, Tumbo does 
not guarantee employment, funding, acceptance or selection by any organisation.
All recruitment and selection decisions remain solely the responsibility of the respective 
opportunity providers.
Tumbo's role is to simplify access to opportunities by connecting opportunity seekers with 
trusted organisations.
3. POPIA Compliance
To comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the application should 
include the following:
User Consent
Users must explicitly consent to the collection and processing of their personal information 
before creating an account or submitting applications.
Privacy Notice
The platform should clearly explain:
What information is collected.
Why it is collected.
How it will be used.
Who it may be shared with.
How long it will be retained.
How it will be protected.
Data Protection
Personal information should be securely stored and encrypted where appropriate.
Access must be restricted to authorised personnel and approved system processes.
User Rights
Users should be able to:
View their personal information.
Update inaccurate information.
Request deletion of their account (subject to legal requirements).
Withdraw consent where applicable.
Sharing Information
Where application details are submitted directly to an organisation, users must be informed 
that only the information required for that application will be shared, and only with their 
===== PAGE 10 =====
consent.
Expected Outcome
These enhancements will create a seamless application journey, strengthen user trust, 
improve legal compliance with POPIA, and position Tumbo as a reliable bridge between 
opportunity seekers and opportunity providers.
